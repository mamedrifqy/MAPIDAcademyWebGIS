What will be worked on?

Province Etymology

This project aims to map the etymology of the names of provinces in Indonesia.

Data used

  • Spatial Data:
    • Provincial Administrative Boundary Data from BIG (Geospatial Information Agency)
  • Non-Spatial Data:
    • Etymology Data from Desk Study Results

The results of this project are expected to provide useful information for the community who need information related to the etymology of provinces in Indonesia
